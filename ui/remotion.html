How it works
  1. Write the topic, choose the duration and the video options (loop, "Intip yuk!", music, voice apps).
  2. Copy the brief into Claude Code opened in the klipian folder. It searches the web, writes the story and makes the video folder.
  3. Choose the folder it made and load the story, check every claim with its source, then choose a title.
  4. Make the script for the voice app, import the voice and match it, look at the preview, then Render. The result goes to Clips, ready to upload.
