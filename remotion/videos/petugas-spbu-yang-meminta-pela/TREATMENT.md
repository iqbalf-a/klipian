# Treatment: Petugas SPBU yang Meminta Pelanggan Menyentuh Logam Dulu (ricks, stage 1)

Kept next to the story until stage 2 builds `videos/petugas-spbu-yang-meminta-pela/`; then it moves there as `TREATMENT.md` (a video folder without `index.tsx` would fail `tests/videos.test.ts`).

## Benchmark
Learn: RicksClips3000 "The Burglar Who Only Moved Furniture" and "Why Cashiers Keep Winning The Lottery": one deadpan expert who refuses to explain, a loud doubter, the turn shown as one clear physical event, the doubters ending up doing the very thing they mocked, held on screen before the loop. Also the way their sparks and flashes are a single bright frame, not a long effect.
Do not take: their characters, their sets, their melodies, their logos.

## Structures
1. Opening: close on Bu Yati's hand holding the nozzle, a queue behind. Turn: a spark jumps from Bang Toni's finger. Ending: Bang Toni slaps his door first, then she lifts the nozzle.
2. Opening: a wide high shot of the long queue and its horns. Turn: Bang Toni's hair lifts in slow motion as he steps out. Ending: Bu Yati herself touches the door, then pumps.
3. Opening: a low shot through the hose at the face-off over the nozzle. Turn: the picture goes quiet for half a second, then one blue spark whitens the frame. Ending: the whole queue of drivers pats its doors one after another, the last shot returns to the face-off.

## Choice
3. Structures 1 and 2 are the first ideas and end on one person (the cliche); 3 ends on the whole queue, so "the doubters get what they laughed at" is a picture, and its quiet-then-flash turn gives the silence before the turn (B71 a). The hook frame is still the face-off with Bu Yati holding the nozzle (B41).
