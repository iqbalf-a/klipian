# Pilot 2 "Kenapa hujan turun?" (SVG 2.5D): plan for klipian eksekusi (approved by ian)

Written by klipian motion (planning only). Pilot 1 (colokan) is done and on origin/main; the Three.js spike ended with "stay with SVG for object scenes with few overlapping parts" and rain is exactly that case, so pilot 2 uses the **SVG 2.5D kit**. The approved look reference is `workspace/channel-art/klipian-intip/hujan-25d.png` (generator `make_hujan25d.py`, read only; do not write to `workspace/`): an isometric sea block (blue, three tones), a land block (grass top, soil sides, cone trees), a bright sun with rays, a white cloud and a grey rain cloud (three tones, layered puffs), vapour wisps rising, dots, rain streaks, numbered pointer labels, the kicker and headline, a karaoke caption.

This plan has **two phases with a stop between them**, so ian can look at the new nature kit before the video is built on it.

In `/remotion`, make a new video: topic "Kenapa hujan turun?", 45 seconds. Copy the brief to Claude or ChatGPT, paste the JSON back, check every claim in the fact gate. ## Phase 1: the nature kit (library assets), then STOP and report
Rules of the road (from `DESIGN.md`, sections 2.5D, 5, highlights, 10, 11, 12 and the pilot 1 rules): three tones per object by the tone rule; the nature colours are the only "meaning colours" allowed besides the brand: **blue = water, green = plants, brown = soil** (and white/grey = cloud), each with three tones; red only for heat or danger; yellow only for the sun, current and highlights; nothing drawn over what hides it (depth order); every highlight lives on its object with a meaning; check every moving part in its in-between states.

1. **Tokens** (`src/tokens.ts`): add to `ISO` three-tone sets for `water`, `soil`, `grass`, `leaf`, `cloudWhite`, `cloudGrey`, `sun` (take the numbers from `make_hujan25d.py`: water W1..W3 (120,182,226) (78,146,204) (52,112,170); land L1..L3; grass GR1..GR2; adjust to three tones each, light on top, mid on the lower left, dark on the lower right). A test: these colours are used only by the nature assets (not by any other component).
2. **Components** (`src/components/IsoNature.tsx`, new file, exported from `src/design/index.ts`), all with props for state (0..1) so a plan can drive them and the loop works:
   - `IsoSea`: the sea block (top plane blue, two sides), `ripples` as arcs **on the top plane** (clipped to the top face, `OnPlane`), `sun` glint, a `heat` shimmer.
   - `IsoLand`: the land block (grass top with a lighter edge, soil sides), `IsoTree` cones with a trunk (three tones), `wet` (0..1) darkens the grass and soil slightly after rain.
   - `IsoSun`: disc with a light and a mid tone, rays that pulse; `IsoCloud`: puffy cloud of layered ellipses with the three-tone rule (dark underside, mid body, light top bumps), `dark` (0..1) turns it from white to grey, `grow` scales it, with its puffs breathing slowly.
   - `Vapour` (wisps rising from the sea, start **inside** the water surface and fade out toward the cloud), `Droplets` (small blue dots that gather near the cloud's underside), `Rain` (streaks falling from the **underside** of the cloud, clipped so they start behind the cloud's edge), `Splash` (small rings and drops where rain meets the sea or the land, on the correct plane), `Runoff` (a thin blue stream on the land block toward the sea, optional).
   - All have a `loopPhase` or are periodic so the video's last frame can equal its first.
3. **Depth-order table** in the file header: for each pair that touches or overlaps, which one is in front (cloud in front of sun, rain behind the cloud's lower edge, trees on the land top plane, vapour behind the cloud, ripples clipped to the water top, splash on the right plane). Tests check the draw order from the source (as the colokan tests do) and that every clipped part stays inside its face.
4. **Library page**: a nature tile set on a new `AssetSheetNature` (or a section of the asset sheet; do not break `AssetSheetIso`), an entry in the catalog (`src/catalog.ts`), `npm run assets-sheet` output. A `NatureDemo` composition (6 to 8 seconds: sea evaporates, cloud forms and darkens, rain falls, splash) registered in `Root.tsx` and allowed in `render.mjs`.
5. **Sounds** (`sfx/synth.mjs`, synthesised, CC0, listed in `sfx/LICENSES.md` and in `src/catalog.ts` `SFX`): `rain-patter` (soft filtered noise bursts), `drip` (a small bubble drop), `bubble-rise` (a gentle rising tone for the vapour). Keep the table style of the existing sounds and the "at most 3 at once" rule.
6. **Checks**: `npm run check && npm test`; the in-between states strip for moving parts (cloud `dark` 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1; rain start frames; vapour entering the cloud; splash rings) and a contact sheet; zoom 3x at the cloud edge where the rain starts, at the sea's top edge where the ripples and vapour start, and at the tree/land edge.
7. **Report after phase 1** (stop here): the kit in a contact sheet next to `hujan-25d.png` (ian compares the look), the tests, the new rules for `DESIGN.md` (write them in, a section "Nature kit"), the new sounds, and the render time of `NatureDemo`. Do not start phase 2 until ian says so.

## Phase 2: the video (after ian's OK and after the story exists)
1. `npm run new-video kenapa-hujan-turun "<path of the story.json>"` as in `DESIGN.md` section 0. All screen text comes from the story (never typed in code); the claims and their sources come from the story; the numbers only if a checked claim has them.
2. **One continuous take**, no cuts, like pilot 1, with the **"Intip" lens used where it earns its place** (look into the cloud to see the tiny droplets joining and growing heavy; the lab background for everything inside the lens). Suggested beats (adapt to the story's real beats and durations): hook (rain falls on a window-less landscape, a question) → small "Intip yuk!" sting (small mascot, 200 to 240 px, no veil) → the sun heats the sea, vapour rises (label "penguapan") → the camera follows the vapour up to the cloud → through the lens: droplets form and join (label "kondensasi") → back out, the cloud darkens and the rain falls on land and sea (label "hujan") → the rain runs back to the sea → the cycle repeats: **loop-back ending** (the last frame equals the first: same camera, same sea, same cloud, no mascot, no end text, periodic ambient motion, no music fade-out).
3. A pure `plan.ts` (times from the narration's word times, cameras, states, boxes of every frame), `index.tsx`, `sfx.ts` (sound on the frame of its picture; music `bed-calm`), `tests/<slug>.test.ts` (layout conflicts every 3 frames, text from the story, smooth cameras, sfx on frames, depth order, loop equality, mascot small, no outro text).
4. The standing pilot 1 rules apply: highlight shapes live on their object's plane with a meaning (a number label from story words, not a repeat of the card), cables/strokes are never rigid (the rain and the vapour are curved and varied, not ruler-straight), parts that go behind something are hidden by it, joints are smooth, check **every moving part in its in-between states** and give the **exact times** you checked. Cloud and rain shapes are not allowed to look like flat stickers: layered puffs with the tone rule.
5. **Verify before reporting**: `npm run check && npm test`; stills of every beat; frames around every camera move and the lens in/out; the loop numbers (first vs last frame PSNR/SSIM and the seam clip); render the MP4 with sound (check CPU first) and report render time and the sound-event list; score it against the 8 quality-bar points (honestly, saying what is missing).
