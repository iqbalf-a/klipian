# Safe areas for YouTube Shorts (first), TikTok and Instagram Reels (plan for klipian eksekusi, approved by ian)

Written by klipian motion (planning only). Ian (2026-10-31): "position it according to the safe areas, especially YouTube, TikTok and Instagram; YouTube is the priority." Today the kit has one safe area (`SAFE` in `src/tokens.ts`: x 60..950, y 120..1632, "the Shorts interface covers the bottom 15 % and the right 12 %"), the caption box ends at 1620 and the top text starts at y 96.

## What the sources say (read 2026-10-31; **none of the three platforms publishes official pixel margins for organic posts**; every number below is a third-party estimate, and they disagree)
- **YouTube Shorts** (priority). Estimates: about 160 px covered at the top, about 320 px at the bottom (title, channel, subscribe), about 150 px on the right (the action buttons; one tool puts their left edge near x 875). Sources: [kreatli](https://kreatli.com/guides/youtube-shorts-safe-zone), [shortsafezone](https://shortsafezone.com/) ("observed approximations, not an official specification"), [creamate](https://test.creamate.ai/en/tools/youtube-shorts-safe-zone), [postplanify](https://postplanify.com/tools/youtube-shorts-safe-zone-checker).
- **TikTok.** TikTok gives its in-feed safe zone only as template files (ads), not numbers. Organic estimates: top about 130, bottom about 320 (ads about 480 or more), right about 140, left about 44. Sources: [imagevideofit](https://imagevideofit.com/guides/tiktok-safe-zone), [kreateflo](https://kreateflo.com/blog/why-vertical-caption-placement-matters-for-tiktok-and-shorts-safe-zones), [yosefk](https://yosefk.me/blog/tiktok-safe-zone/).
- **Instagram Reels.** The Reels **ads** split (top 14 % about 270 px, bottom 35 % about 670 px, sides 6 % about 65 px) is repeated by several sites; organic Reels have no published margins; estimates for organic range from a bottom of about 310 to 420 px and a top of about 108 to 220 px. Sources: [zeely](https://zeely.ai/blog/master-instagram-safe-zones/), [houseofmarketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories-reels/), [kreatli](https://kreatli.com/guides/instagram-reels-safe-zone).
- **Consequence:** our caption at 1620 sits **inside** YouTube's estimated bottom band (1600 to 1920) by 20 px, and our top text (y 96) is inside the top band of all three. These are the two things to fix first.

## The decision (YouTube first, the others checked)
1. **Platform profiles** in `src/tokens.ts`: `SAFE_PLATFORMS = { youtube, tiktok, instagram }`, each `{ top, bottom, left, right }` in video px **with a comment naming the sources and the date and saying "estimate, not official"**. Values to start with:
   - `youtube`: top 170, bottom 1600, left 60, right 920.
   - `tiktok`: top 140, bottom 1600, left 60, right 930.
   - `instagram` (organic): top 220, bottom 1500, left 65, right 1015 (the ads profile, bottom 1250, is listed but not used).
2. **The working safe area `SAFE` = the YouTube profile** (top 170, bottom 1600, left 60, right 920). Everything the layout keeps safe follows it: text, the mascot, people, labels, badges, source cards, the caption.
3. **Caption:** every variant (`classic`, `wide`, `punch`) ends at **`bottom` 1590** (10 px above YouTube's 1600) and its box stays inside x 60..920 (the `wide` text width shrinks to fit; `classic` 770 already fits). Rule B22 is updated (1620 to 1590) and the reason is written (the YouTube estimate).
4. **Top text:** `TOP_TEXT.y` from 96 to **170** (the kicker and the headline start below the top icons); the banner zone, the `bounds()` and the zones that hang from it move with it. The objects keep their places unless `conflicts()` says otherwise.
5. **TikTok and Instagram are checked, not obeyed:** `style-report` (and a small `npm run safe-check -- <slug>`) lists, per platform, what text or key element of the video falls inside that platform's covered bands, at sampled frames (every 3 frames): "Instagram: caption 1490 to 1590 is inside the bottom band (organic estimate 1500)". A flag, never a failure. A story field `platforms` (optional, default `["youtube"]`): when a video lists `instagram`, the layout obeys the strictest of the listed profiles (a cross-post mode: the caption bottom would then be 1490).
6. **Pilot 1 and the other videos** follow the new `SAFE` (ian asked for the change for all): re-run the layout on them, fix any conflict in their `plan.ts` (the label zones, the stage drop of `ngantuk-pegang-hp` which assumed 1632: its bed base must stay inside 1600 or be cut by the floor; reduce `stageDrop` if needed), and show before | after stills.
