# Variety inside the style: music, backgrounds, stage shapes, props (plan for klipian eksekusi, approved by ian)

Written by klipian motion (planning only). Ian (2026-10-28), after watching two renders (`workspace/profiles/klipian-intip/out/motion`: "Hujan buatan..." and "Ngantuk pas scroll..."): the sound effects are fine, but **the background music, the assets and the screen background design have very little variety**, and the music is "sleepy, not cheerful, not happy". Work in **four stages**, one report after each. Stage 1 stops for ian's ears (he picks); stages 2 to 4 continue one after another with a report each.

## What I measured (klipian motion, from the two renders and the code)
- **Music:** only two beds exist, `bed-calm` and `bed-tech` (`sfx/music.mjs`, `MUSIC` in `src/catalog.ts`): both a 24 s loop, **80 BPM**, the **same chords (Am F C G)**, sines and plucks. Both renders use `bed-calm`; a 45 to 53 s video repeats the same loop twice. A minor, slow, soft bed is why it sounds sleepy.
- **Backgrounds:** `BACKGROUNDS = room, black, brand, lab`. **All 8 beats of both stories use `brand`** (cream, a dot pattern, a big yellow spot behind the object); the only other look is the dark lens beat (`lab`).
- **Layout and assets:** every beat is one isometric stage block in the middle, one hero on it, the same title top-left and the caption box at the bottom; props repeat (the same cloud, the same slab). Consistent, but too uniform across videos.
- The style bible (`DESIGN.md` 0b) locks consistency but has **no rule for allowed variety**, so nothing in the pipeline varies.

## What stays fixed (do not touch)
The **sound effects and their mix** (rule B15, `soundHash.json`): ian still approves them. **The text style** (B17). The isometric camera, the three-tone rule, the "Intip yuk!" sting and the loop-back (the musts). The lens look. Existing videos (pilot 1 `kenapa-colokan-panas`, `ngantuk-pegang-hp`) must still render and pass their tests unchanged: new choices apply to **new** stories; an old story with no new field behaves as before.

## Stage 1: cheerful music beds (ian listens, then picks)
Goal: **cheerful, happy, light**, still quiet under the narration.
- **New beds (at least four)**, made the same way (synthesised in `sfx/music.mjs`, CC0, listed in `sfx/LICENSES.md`), each a seamless loop (a note that ends after the last sample continues at the first; `LEN` may be 16, 24 or 32 s, always a whole number of bars): `bed-sunny` (major key, about 108 BPM, marimba or pluck melody, a soft shaker and a bouncy bass), `bed-bounce` (about 116 BPM, staccato bass, light claps, playful), `bed-glow` (about 96 BPM, warm chords with a bell arpeggio: friendly, for everyday objects), `bed-curious` (about 100 BPM, a Dorian or mixolydian feel, playful and a little mysterious: for myth checks and "how does it work"). **Different chord progressions per bed** (not Am F C G); at most one bed may use the old one.
- **Measurable**, written in the report next to `bed-calm` and `bed-tech` (a small table): BPM, the key and mode, the onset rate (notes and hits per second), the spectral centroid and the integrated loudness. A cheerful bed must be **faster than 95 BPM, in a major or a bright mode, with a higher onset rate and centroid than `bed-calm`**; the loudness stays in the same range as the existing beds, and the voice band (1 to 4 kHz) must not be louder than in `bed-calm` by more than 2 dB, so the narration stays clear.
- **Do not edit `bed-calm` and `bed-tech`** (they stay, marked legacy: `soundHash.json` keeps their hashes; new beds get **new entries**, nothing existing is rewritten; a test checks that the old entries are unchanged). Do not touch any sound effect.
- **Wire them:** `MUSIC` and `MUSIC_NAMES` in `src/catalog.ts` get the new beds (the `MUSIC` mix levels for them, judged against the pilot 1 mix, with the same ducking or level rules as the old beds); the schema `music.track` accepts them; **`klipian/motion.py` `MUSIC_TRACKS`** and the panel's music select list them with Indonesian labels (Ceria / Pantul / Hangat / Penasaran / Tenang (lama) / Teknologi (lama) / Tanpa musik); **the default for a new video becomes `sunny`**; the brief names the beds and when each fits (a cause or an object: sunny or glow; a process or a gadget: bounce; a myth check or a how-does-it-work: curious). Old projects keep their track.
- **Ian listens:** write each new bed (and `bed-calm` for comparison) as `out/music/<name>.wav` (the full loop, plus a 30 s version that plays the loop twice across the seam) and a short note of what each is. **Stop and report** (do not make a bed the default until ian says which one(s) he likes; if ian picks different ones, change the default and the brief text).
- Optional if cheap: a simple **arrangement over the video** (a thinner start, full at the result beat, thinner for the tips) by volume steps keyed to the beat kinds; otherwise leave it as a note for later.

## Stage 2: background themes
- Keep one family (the dotted spotlight look and the dark lens) but add **themes**: a registry `THEMES` in `src/tokens.ts` with a base tint, a spot colour, a pattern, all tokens: `cream` (today, the default), `sky` (a light blue), `mint` (a light green), `peach` (a warm light), `night` (a dark blue with a warm spot, for night or space topics). The dot pattern may switch between `dots`, `grid` and `stripes` (soft) per theme.
- A **story field** `"theme"` (optional, an enum; missing means `cream`), and **one theme per video** (the hook, the explain beats, the result, the tips and the loop-back outro share it, so the loop stays seamless); the lens `lab` beat stays dark in every theme. The brief asks the AI to choose a theme by kind of fact (object, process, cause, myth) and says that two videos in a row should not share one.
- **Checks:** every theme keeps the text readable (the black caption box and the white text are unchanged; labels and kickers pass a contrast check against the theme's base), the objects' tone triples still read on it (a frame check on the two renders re-rendered in each theme), no colour literals (tokens only: `styleCheck`).
- Show it: a contact sheet of one existing story in all five themes (`out/themes/`) for ian to judge.

## Stage 3: stage variants
- Keep the isometric camera. Add a **registry of stage shapes** (`src/components/IsoStage.tsx` or where the slab lives): the current slab, a **round plate**, a **long strip** (for a flow or a process), a **stepped platform**, a **floating island** (a slab with a soft under-shadow) and the **placements** `center`, `left`, `right` (offset hero with the label zone clear), each declared with its footprint so the layout checks (`conflicts()`) still pass.
- A story field `"stage"` (optional: `slab` default, `round`, `strip`, `steps`, `island`) per video; a video may use a **second shape in the look-closer beats** only through its `plan.ts`. The hero keeps 60 to 70 % of the screen width at the main shot (the bible rule), the title and the caption box stay where they are.
- The brief says when to use each, and `style-report` prints the shape and the theme of the video.

## Stage 4: a bigger shared library and the variety rule
- Add a small set of **reusable props** that everyday topics keep needing, each as a kit component with its dictionary mark and detail number, flat tones and no outline: a **table or desk**, a **door**, a **window**, a **lamp**, a **cup**, a **house**, a **tree** in two variants, a **cloud** in two variants (clear sky and storm: reuse the cloud of the rain kit), a **phone** (from the sleep video), and a **plant**. Reuse before drawing: only props that two stories already drew or that a topic list needs now; **at most 10**, each tested (tones, tokens, occlusion order) and shown in the mark sheet.
- **Variety rule (B21, new)**, in `DESIGN.md` and checked by `style-report`: it lists the music bed, the theme and the stage shape of the last five videos (from their `story.json` and `meta.json`) and **flags a video that repeats the bed and the theme of the one before it**; a flag, not a failure (ian decides). Add the rows to the rule log: the cause (ian, 2026-10-28, two renders), the measures above, and the amendment to **B15**: the sound effects and their mix are locked; the music beds are an open set (old beds stay as they are, new beds are added after ian listens).
